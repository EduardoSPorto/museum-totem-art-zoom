# Information Needed from Belani

The following information is needed to complete the material data displayed by the interface.

## 1. Material-to-Color Mapping

For each relevant color/region of the painting, identify the material or pigment actually associated with that region.

Please provide:

* **Color / region**
* **Pigment or material**
* **Chemical composition**
* **Chemical formula**
* **Basis for the identification** (for example: analysis, historical documentation, comparison, etc.)

This is especially important for:

* Red
* Blue
* White
* Yellow
* Black

The current documentation explicitly supports **yellow → cadmium sulfide (CdS)**, but does not provide an equivalent verified mapping for the other colors.

## 9. Cadmium Yellow — Additional Information

Cadmium yellow is the best-documented material in the current research, and its basic identification is already sufficient:

* Cadmium Yellow
* Cadmium sulfide
* CdS

However, the interface would still benefit from confirmation of the following:

* Exact material/composition of the cadmium yellow used in the relevant artwork region
* Whether the material should be represented strictly as **CdS**, or whether the documented historical compositional variations are relevant to this specific material
* Appropriate chemical structure/model for visualization
* Confirmation that the yellow region of the specific painting is the region associated with cadmium yellow
